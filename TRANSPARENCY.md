# ProofTube Protocol — Financial Transparency & Governance

ProofTube is a 100% free and open-source public good licensed under MIT / CC0. To ensure complete community trust, radical accountability, and open public auditing, all protocol funding, grants, community contributions, and developer bounty payouts adhere to strict transparent governance standards.

---

## 1. Custody & Governance Transition Plan
* **Current Phase:** During initial project architecture and grant application setup, repository stewardship is managed by the lead protocol steward.
* **Multisig Migration:** Upon initial grant approval and the onboarding of core maintainers/advisors, treasury management will transition to a 2-of-3 Bitcoin Multi-Signature Vault so no single individual can move or disburse project funds unilaterally.

---

## 2. Milestone-Based Grant Disbursements
* All grant funding requested (e.g., OpenSats, Human Rights Foundation) is strictly tied to the public, 4-phase milestone roadmap outlined in `WHITEPAPER.md` (Appendix A).
* Funds remain locked in the treasury until corresponding GitHub Pull Requests (PRs) for each milestone are peer-reviewed, tested, and merged into the main codebase.

---

## 3. Public On-Chain Accounting
* All incoming grants, community donations, and outgoing developer payouts will be tracked publicly on-chain.
* **Treasury Address:** Operational wallet address(es) will be generated and published here upon initial grant approval or campaign launch.
